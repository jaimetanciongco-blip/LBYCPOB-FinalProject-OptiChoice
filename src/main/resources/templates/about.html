Project Overview

OptiChoice is a user-friendly web application which helps students and professionals combat decision fatigue by converting overwhelming choices into straightforward and assured actions. If you have to deal with a number of competing priorities in your academic, professional, financial, health and social life, OptiChoice enables you to assess your options using personal questionnaires that are specific to each area. The system makes use of an intelligent scoring method that takes your preferences into account and employs AI-driven trade-off analysis to provide objective, bias-free recommendations that are directly aligned with your own values and goals. Whether you are picking an internship, choosing a project topic or handling a personal budget, OptiChoice cuts down on mental strain and saves you time by giving clarity to all your decisions.
